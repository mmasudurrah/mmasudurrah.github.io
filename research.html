Research

Learning and reasoning for intelligent autonomy.

I develop reinforcement learning and foundation-model methods for intelligent systems that learn, reason, and act when conditions change.

My work connects adaptive decision making, multimodal reasoning, and human–robot collaboration. Robotics and medicine provide demanding settings for these questions.

Future research

Toward intelligent surgical autonomy.

I aim to connect adaptive learning, multimodal reasoning, and human–robot collaboration in bounded surgical tasks. This direction builds on my robotics and AI work, with clinical translation depending on appropriate partnerships and evaluation. The underlying questions extend to robot learning and intelligent decision systems beyond medicine.
