Md Masudur Rahman

Postdoctoral Researcher, Purdue University

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Edwardson School of Industrial Engineering at Purdue University, working with Juan P. Wachs. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue in 2024, advised by Yexiang Xue.

My research is in reinforcement learning and robot learning, with a focus on surgical and medical robotics. I work on policy optimization methods that remain reliable when conditions change, on vision-language models that support clinical decisions, and on semi-autonomous systems in which a surgeon and a robot share control.

My Robust Policy Optimization (RPO) method is included in the CleanRL and skrl reinforcement learning libraries and is used in Physical Intelligence’s released implementation of real-time action chunking. My work on AI-assisted burn diagnosis has been published in Annals of Surgery, presented as an oral at AAAI/IAAI 2026, and recognized with the Best Paper Award at MIUA 2025.
